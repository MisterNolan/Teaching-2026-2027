The Long Road Home

A Dutch East India Company (VOC) ship sails from Taiwan to the Netherlands, some time in the 1650s.

You are the captain of a VOC ship at Fort Zeelandia on Taiwan. Your job is to bring the ship, the men and the cargo home to the Netherlands.

  • Choose where to sail next. Company ports can feed your men, repair the ship and load cargo.
  • Things will happen on the way. Talk with your crew, then decide.
  • You have 5 minutes. If you are too slow, the ship is posted missing.
  • Watch your men, food and hull. Not every ship gets home.
